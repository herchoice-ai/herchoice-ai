# Security and safeguarding reports

Please **do not** open a public issue for a security vulnerability, a privacy problem, or a safeguarding concern.

Email the maintainers instead: **sulaimanashiru111@gmail.com**, with "HerChoice security" or "HerChoice safeguarding" in the subject line.

We will acknowledge your report within 3 working days and keep you informed until it is resolved.

Please include:
- what you found and where (file, endpoint or message),
- how to reproduce it,
- the possible impact on users.

Never include real user data in a report.
